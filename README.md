# Congo Actions — Philanthropic Causes, Affiliated NGOs & Campaigns Website

Official philanthropic website for **Congo Actions** (*Aider les enfants dans le besoin*), focusing on the humanitarian causes we champion, the verified Congolese NGOs we partner with, and their active fundraising campaigns.

---

## ✨ Website Sections

1. **Hero & Humanitarian Impact Overview**:
   - Emotional storytelling highlighting children, families, and communities across the Democratic Republic of Congo, paired with impact metrics and a live `$ USD ⇄ FC CDF` currency switcher.
2. **Causes We Champion (`#causes-categories`)**:
   - WhyDonate-inspired cause category pillars (*Orphan Care & Child Nutrition*, *Maternal & Emergency Healthcare*, *Clean Water & Cholera Prevention*, and *Youth Schools & Digital Skills*) that filter and jump directly to matching campaigns.
3. **Active Fundraising Campaigns (`#active-causes`)**:
   - Filterable grid of verified campaigns with funding progress bars, supporter counts, full story modals, and direct links to donate via the mobile app.
4. **Verified Partner Directory (`#affiliated-ngos`)**:
   - Directory of affiliated Congolese NGOs (**Fondation Panzi**, **Kivu Clean Water Initiative**, **Orphelinat de Kinshasa**, and **Sauvons Congo**) with NGO profile modals and our 4-step legal vetting guarantee (*Arrêté Ministériel* & *Certificat F92*).
5. **Donation Models & Payment Options Carousel Slider (`#donation-types`)**:
   - Overview of our four giving models (*Pooled Emergency Relief Funds*, *Direct Single-NGO Campaigns*, *Monthly Recurring Pledges*, and *Dual Currency USD & CDF*) paired with an interactive **Carousel Slider** showcasing DRC Mobile Money (*Vodacom M-Pesa*, *Orange Money*, *Airtel Money*, *Afrimoney*) and International Diaspora options (*Apple Pay*, *Google Pay*, *Visa & Mastercard*).
6. **Real Stories from the Field (`#impact-stories`)**:
   - Highlights on-the-ground field updates and verified supplier receipts shared by partner NGOs.
7. **Brief Mobile App Overview & Shared Pool Calculator (`#mobile-app`)**:
   - Concise section at the end of the page explaining what the mobile app does, how to download it on iOS and Android, and a simple interactive preview showing how a shared gift is divided across three causes.
