OUR TRUST GUARANTEE

How Every Affiliated NGO is Vetted

Before any organization can raise funds on Congo Actions, our compliance team verifies four mandatory legal documents.

01. Arrêté Ministériel Official DRC Ministerial Decree authorizing non-profit operations.
02. Certificat F92 Ministry of Justice registration confirming active charitable standing.
03. Notarized Bylaws Audited organizational charter, mission scope, and board governance.
04. Identity & Bank Audit Verified director identity and official NGO bank or Mobile Money account.
DONATION MODELS & PAYMENT OPTIONS

Flexible Ways to Give from the DRC or Abroad

Congo Actions makes it effortless for both local citizens in the Congo and international supporters across the diaspora to back the causes they care about most.

Multi-NGO Shared Impact 01

Pooled Emergency Relief Funds

Want to help multiple urgent causes at once? When you give to a Pooled Fund (like the Eastern DRC Emergency & Health Relief Pool), your single donation is automatically shared among three partner NGOs delivering healthcare, clean water, and orphan care.

  • ✓ 40% to Fondation Panzi (Maternal & Surgical Care)
  • ✓ 35% to Kivu Clean Water (Solar Water Wells)
  • ✓ 25% to Orphelinat de Kinshasa (Child Nutrition)
100% Dedicated Cause 02

Direct Single-NGO Campaigns

Choose a specific project close to your heart—such as drilling solar boreholes in Mugunga camp, feeding 140 orphans in Ngaliema, or equipping youth computer labs in Masina—and direct 100% of your gift to that NGO's campaign.

  • ✓ Dedicated directly to your chosen NGO project
  • ✓ Option to cover small payment processing fees
  • ✓ Receive direct field updates as milestones are met
Sustained Monthly Care 03

Monthly Recurring Pledges

Orphanages, hospitals, and schools rely on steady monthly support to plan ahead. Set up a recurring monthly gift by card from abroad, or receive a simple monthly Mobile Money reminder on your phone in the DRC to renew with one tap.

  • ✓ Provide reliable monthly meals & medicine
  • ✓ Pause, adjust, or resume anytime in the app
  • ✓ Earn community impact supporter badges
Local & Global Currencies 04

Dual Currency ($ USD & FC CDF)

Whether you prefer to give in US Dollars ($ USD) or Congolese Francs (FC CDF), Congo Actions supports both currencies seamlessly so every donor can contribute in the currency they use every day.

  • ✓ Instant $ USD ⇄ FC CDF currency switching
